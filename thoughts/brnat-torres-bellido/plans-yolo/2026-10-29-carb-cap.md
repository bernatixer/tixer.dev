# Derived maximum-carb progress bar

## Goal

Show today's carbs against a derived daily cap when the current food-day calorie target and body weight are known. The cap reserves calories for the fixed 1.5 g/kg protein target and a 0.6 g/kg minimum fat allowance; the remaining calories are carbs.

## Decisions

- Use 0.6 g/kg as the minimum-fat allowance — a simple conservative floor that leaves the remaining calorie budget for carbs.
- Keep the existing protein target fixed at 1.5 g/kg — current product behavior and tests establish it as the source of truth.
- Suppress the cap without a positive intake target or body weight — presenting a guessed target would be misleading.
